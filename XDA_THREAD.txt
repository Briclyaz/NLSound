[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=#0099CC]What is NLSound (AROMA-Next Edition)?[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]
[B]NLSound — AROMA-Next Edition[/B] is the official [B]AROMA-Next-Installer (ANI) V26.11-RC5[/B] edition of [URL=https://github.com/Briclyaz/NLSound]Briclyaz/NLSound v4.5[/URL]. It removes MMT-Extended and the legacy 200-line bash Volume Key menu in favor of [B]ONE single [ICODE]aroma-config[/ICODE][/B] that delivers:
[LIST]
[*][B][COLOR=#0099CC]Custom Recovery (TWRP / OrangeFox / AERA / PitchBlack — BOOTMODE=0):[/COLOR][/B] Full 60–120Hz [B]AMOLED Modern Material 3 You Touch GUI[/B] (Monochrome White default + 8 Live Accents, 17 redesigned icons, Top Status Bar, 1-tap stay-open Appearance Bottom Sheet, lossless PNG screenshots) with a live SmartPA/DAC hardware dashboard, [B]1-Tap Audiophile Preset[/B], [B]1-Tap Profile Restore[/B], and all 15 audio tweaks cleanly grouped into [B]4 visual screens[/B]!
[*][B][COLOR=#00AA55]Magisk / KernelSU / APatch App (BOOTMODE=1):[/COLOR][/B] Automatic native C [B]Volume Key Selector[/B] rendered straight from the exact same [ICODE]aroma-config[/ICODE] — complete with 48-column UTF-8 word-wrapping, [B]Go Back navigation[/B] ([ICODE]<- Go Back[/ICODE] / [ICODE]VOL-[/ICODE] long-press), and [ICODE]--preset[/ICODE] automation!
[*][B][COLOR=#9B59B6]Integrated AROMA-Next-Filemanager (ANF):[/COLOR][/B] Built right into the Setup Hub and 3-Dot Menu with 1-tap [B]Bootloop-Saver (Disable ALL Modules)[/B], Module Manager, Storage/System browser, ZIP Inspector, and Quick Reboot.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B][COLOR=#0099CC]Audio Highlights (NLSound v4.5)[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Smooth Volume & Hardware Gain:[/B] 30 / 50 / 100 media volume steps, independent speaker/wired mixer preamp gain (78–108), and microphone DEC sensitivity tuning.
[*][B]True Hi-Fi & Sub-Bass Restoration:[/B] Lowers headphone High-Pass Filter (HPF) down to [B]4 Hz[/B], unlocks [ICODE]DIRECT_PCM[/ICODE] bit-perfect routing (Poweramp, UAPP, Neutron), and supports up to 24/32-bit PCM & 96/192/384 kHz output.
[*][B]Dynamics & Wireless:[/B] Disables DRC, softclip, compander & low-battery throttling; unlocks [B]Bluetooth SBC HD Dual Channel[/B], aptX Adaptive & disables buggy Absolute Volume.
[*][B]Effects, Dolby Atmos & Hardware Tinymix:[/B] Partial or full effects cleanup, clean linear Dolby Atmos DAX profile, Qualcomm ACDB options, and automatic model-specific [ICODE]tinymix[/ICODE] hardware registers (Cirrus Logic, TI TAS, NXP TFA, Awinic, Qualcomm WSA/WCD, ESS Sabre, AKM).
[*][B]Shared [ICODE]settings.nls[/ICODE] Profiles:[/B] Profiles saved in Recovery and profiles saved in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch are 100% interchangeable for 1-tap restore on future updates!
[/LIST]
